About Me

With a Master’s in Information Systems from the University of Florida and global experience at Experian across North America, India, and EMEA, my professional background has been rooted in technology and data. After Experian, my growing interest in Indian equities became a serious pursuit. I immersed myself in the markets through regular market-hours exposure, independent learning, Zerodha Varsity, extensive reading, and hands-on technical analysis using TradingView.

But I didn’t want that knowledge to remain theoretical. I wanted to build with it. What began as an effort to better understand the markets ultimately led me to conceptualize and build products that bring together financial markets, data, and technology.
